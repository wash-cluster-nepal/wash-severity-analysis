How to read this dashboard

Municipality colours summarize the selected aggregate indicator. Points are individual assessed WASH systems with usable coordinates; schemes without coordinates remain included in KPIs and charts. D0–D5 expresses physical damage and S1–S5 expresses site safety. These infrastructure views complement, but do not replace, municipality severity analysis.

Data interpretation: the 90 PDNA scheme records are an assessed infrastructure dataset, not a complete inventory of every WASH system in each municipality. Scheme beneficiary populations can overlap and must not be interpreted as unique people. Eight assessed schemes currently have no usable public map coordinate; they remain in all aggregate statistics. Facility-level contacts, assessor details and other non-public fields are excluded.
